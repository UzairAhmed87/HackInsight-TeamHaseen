# Draft PAQI methodology inquiry — not sent

**To:** Pakistan Air Quality Initiative (PAQI), via its publicly listed contact channel  
**Subject:** Methodology and provenance for the Karachi daily PM2.5 series (2016–2024)

Dear PAQI team,

We are preparing a student project using the versioned Karachi daily PM2.5 series in *Unveiling Karachi's Air: A Scientific Foundation for a Clean Air City* (Zenodo DOI [10.5281/zenodo.15845428](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15845428)). The README describes daily 24-hour averages from the PAQI monitoring network. To describe and evaluate the series accurately, could you clarify:

1. Which stations and instruments contributed, including their locations and operating dates? Did the network or contributing monitors change during 2016–2024?
2. Does each published daily value represent one monitor, a selected monitor, or an aggregation across monitors? If aggregated, what is the aggregation and completeness rule?
3. What local-time timezone and exact start/end hours define a daily 24-hour value?
4. What calibration, collocation, QA/QC, validation, maintenance, and instrument-change procedures apply? Were they consistent throughout the series?
5. What do blank PM2.5 cells mean? Were readings omitted, invalidated, or unavailable for another reason, and are additional quality flags or excluded observations available?
6. Did the series definition, instrumentation, processing, or quality-control procedure change during the period? If so, could you identify the dates and changes?

We will describe the values only as the published PAQI Karachi series unless the documentation supports a more specific interpretation. We will retain the stated CC BY 4.0 attribution and will not imply PAQI endorsement.

Thank you for your time.

**Status:** Draft only. No message has been sent and no response has been received.
